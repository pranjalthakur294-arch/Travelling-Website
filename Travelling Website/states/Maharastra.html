About Maharashtra

Maharashtra is one of the most popular travel states in India. From the energetic streets of Mumbai to the cool hills of Lonavala and Mahabaleshwar, this state offers a beautiful mix of city life, beaches, historic forts, caves, pilgrimage sites and natural landscapes. It is perfect for travellers who want culture, adventure, relaxation and local food all in one destination.

Famous Food of Maharashtra

Maharashtra offers a delicious mix of street food, festive sweets and traditional dishes that reflect the culture and flavour of the state.

Vada Pav

The most iconic street food of Maharashtra, especially popular in Mumbai. It is spicy, tasty and perfect for a quick bite while travelling.

Misal Pav

A spicy and flavorful dish made with sprouts, curry, farsan and pav. It is one of the most loved breakfast foods in many cities of Maharashtra.

Puran Poli

A sweet flatbread stuffed with jaggery and lentil filling, commonly prepared during festivals and special family occasions.

Modak

A traditional sweet dish strongly associated with Lord Ganesha and widely enjoyed during Ganesh Chaturthi celebrations.

Festivals of Maharashtra

Maharashtra celebrates many vibrant festivals with devotion, food, music and local traditions, making the travel experience even more memorable.

Ganesh Chaturthi

The most famous festival of Maharashtra, celebrated with grand Ganpati idols, music, decorations and cultural programs across the state.

Gudi Padwa

The Marathi New Year festival, marked by colorful rangoli, festive clothes, traditional food and the raising of the gudi outside homes.

Diwali

The festival of lights is celebrated with diyas, sweets, decorations and joyful family gatherings throughout Maharashtra.

Navratri

A lively festival filled with devotion, music, dance and festive energy that attracts both locals and visitors.

Why Visit?
