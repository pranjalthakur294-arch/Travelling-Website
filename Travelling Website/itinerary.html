Day 7
Day 1 · Tuesday · Delhi
Old Delhi — A City of Layers
Plunge into the sensory overload of Chandni Chowk, Mughal mosques and spice-scented lanes. This city rewards the brave.
08:00
Jama Masjid at sunriseIndia's largest mosque. Climb the south minaret for a panoramic view over Old Delhi's rooftops.
10:00
Cycle rickshaw through Chandni ChowkNavigate the world's most chaotic market street. Stop for paranthe at Paranthe Wali Gali.
14:00
Red Fort & SalimgarhMughal grandeur at its peak. The Sound & Light show in the evening is worth staying for.
20:00
Dinner at Indian AccentDelhi's most acclaimed modern-Indian restaurant. Book 2 weeks ahead. Non-negotiable.
Day 2 · Wednesday · Delhi
New Delhi & Raj-Era Grandeur
Lutyens' wide boulevards, Humayun's Tomb and the bohemian village of Hauz Khas make this a complete contrast to yesterday.
09:00
Humayun's TombThe prototype for the Taj Mahal. Far fewer crowds, just as breathtaking — visit in the morning light.
12:00
Qutb Minar ComplexA UNESCO World Heritage forest of medieval towers and mosques. Rickshaws wait at the gate.
15:30
Hauz Khas VillageDelhi's creative hub — galleries, rooftop cafes and an ancient reservoir. Perfect for sunset chai.
19:00
Train to Agra — Gatimaan ExpressThe fastest train in India. 1hr 40min. Book seats in advance on IRCTC.
Day 3 · Thursday · Agra
The Taj at Dawn
There is nothing that prepares you for the Taj Mahal in real life. Nothing. Wake before sunrise.
05:30
Taj Mahal at sunrise — East GateEnter the east gate for fewer crowds. The marble turns rose-gold as the sun rises. Pure magic.
09:30
Agra FortThe Mughal stronghold where Shah Jahan was imprisoned by his own son, able to see the Taj from his window.
12:30
Lunch at Pind BalluchiReliable North Indian food near Sadar Bazaar. Try the dal makhani and roomali roti.
15:00
Drive to Ranthambore (4 hrs)Hired car recommended — passes through rural Rajasthan landscapes worth seeing.
Day 4 · Friday · Ranthambore
Tiger Country
One of India's premier tiger reserves. Morning and evening safaris give you the best shot at spotting a Bengal tiger in the wild.
06:00
Morning game drive — Zones 3 & 4Tiger sightings are most frequent here. Book a Gypsy (jeep) over a Canter for better access.
10:30
Ranthambore FortA 10th-century fort inside the national park — UNESCO listed, and hauntingly atmospheric.
15:30
Afternoon game drive — Zone 1Lakes frequented by leopards and sloth bears. Crocodiles bask on the banks of Padam Talao.
20:00
Dinner under the stars at your lodgeMost camps serve dinner outdoors around a fire. Rajasthani dal baati churma is the thing to order.
Day 5–6 · Sat–Sun · Jaipur
The Pink City
Jaipur hits you in waves — the blazing terracotta facades, bazaars overflowing with block-print fabrics, and palaces that defy imagination.
08:00
Amber Fort by elephant (or jeep)The hilltop fort overlooking Maota Lake is Rajasthan at its most cinematic. Arrive early.
11:30
City Palace & Jantar MantarThe astronomical observatory built in 1724 still works. Guides bring the giant stone instruments to life.
14:30
Johari Bazaar for gems & textilesJaipur is India's gem capital. Browse kundan jewellery and block-print dupattas.
18:00
Nahargarh Fort at sunsetThe city turns golden below you. Street food stalls serve dal kachori and lassi on the ramparts.
20:30
Dinner at Suvarna Mahal, Rambagh PalaceA former maharaja's palace dining room. Even if just for dessert — come here.

Local tips

🌶 Always say yes when a chai wallah offers you tea. That cup is the best conversation you'll have all day.
🤝 Bargaining is expected in markets — start at 40% of the asking price and meet in the middle warmly.
🚂 Book train tickets on IRCTC at least 2 weeks ahead. Tourist quota seats fill fast in April.
🌡 April is hot. Start all sightseeing by 7am and rest from noon–3pm. Carry an electrolyte sachet.
📸 The Taj Mahal on a full-moon night is open for night viewing — book separately and don't miss it.
