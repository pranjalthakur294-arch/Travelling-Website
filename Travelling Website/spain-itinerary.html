Day 8
Day 1 · Saturday · Madrid
Prado, Tapas & Flamenco
Madrid doesn't do mornings — it does magnificent afternoons that bleed into nights that never quite end. The Prado is your anchor; the rest unfolds.
10:00
Museo del PradoVelázquez, Goya, El Greco — give yourself at least 3 hours. Download the app for audio commentary that actually earns its keep.
14:00
Lunch at Mercado de San MiguelMadrid's iconic iron market hall. Graze on pintxos, jamón ibérico and a cold Mahou beside the locals.
16:30
Retiro Park & Crystal PalaceRow a boat on the lake or simply wander the shaded avenues. The glass-and-iron Crystal Palace catches afternoon light beautifully.
21:00
Flamenco at Corral de la MoreríaMadrid's most storied flamenco tablao. The raw emotion of the performance hits you somewhere you didn't know you had.
Day 2 · Sunday · Madrid
Royals, Rastro & Rooftops
Sunday in Madrid is for the Rastro flea market, the grandeur of the Royal Palace, and watching the city from a rooftop as the sun drops behind the Guadarrama mountains.
09:00
El Rastro Sunday Flea MarketThe largest open-air market in Europe. Vintage posters, ceramics, Franco-era curiosities and outrageously good churros nearby.
12:00
Palacio Real3,418 rooms of Bourbon excess. Even the guided highlights tour will leave your jaw on the marble floor.
15:30
Malasaña neighbourhoodMadrid's coolest barrio — record shops, independent bookstores and terraza bars with afternoon vermouth.
18:30
Sunset at Templo de DebodAn actual Egyptian temple, dismantled and reassembled in Madrid. Sunset here is a Madrid ritual — get there early.
Day 3 · Monday · Toledo
The City of Three Cultures
Toledo is where Christian, Moorish and Jewish Spain coexisted and collided for centuries. The walled hilltop city is one of the most atmospheric places in all of Europe.
08:30
AVE to Toledo (30 min)High-speed train from Madrid Atocha. Arrive before the tour groups swarm the old town — timing everything.
10:00
Toledo CathedralGothic on the grandest possible scale. El Greco's Disrobing of Christ hangs in the sacristy — one of the finest paintings in Spain.
13:00
Barrio Judío & Sinagoga del TránsitoToledo's medieval Jewish quarter. The 14th-century synagogue houses a museum of Sephardic culture.
17:00
Night train to Seville (2.5 hrs)Trade the medieval for the Moorish. Seville awaits with its orange blossom air and flamenco heartbeat.
Day 4 · Tuesday · Seville
Tapas, Tiles & the Alcázar
Seville is Spain distilled to its essence — Moorish palaces, labyrinthine alleys trailing orange blossom, and a city that wakes up at midnight and dances till dawn.
09:00
Real Alcázar de SevillaA UNESCO World Heritage palace of breathtaking Mudéjar craftsmanship. The gardens are worth an hour alone. Game of Thrones was filmed here.
12:30
Barrio de Santa CruzGet lost in the old Jewish quarter — whitewashed walls, azulejo tiles, and hidden plazas with lemon trees.
14:30
Tapas crawl on Calle BetisCross the river to Triana. Espinacas con garbanzos, montaditos and cold Cruzcampo beer on the Guadalquivir riverbank.
19:30
Giralda tower at sunsetClimb the ancient minaret-turned-bell-tower. The panorama over Seville's rooftops and the river is unforgettable.
Days 6–7 · Thu–Fri · Granada
The Alhambra & the Sierra Nevada
Nothing in Europe prepares you for the Alhambra. This Nasrid palace complex — intricate stucco, geometric water channels, views of snow-capped peaks — is the peak of human artistry.
08:00
Alhambra — Nasrid PalacesBook weeks ahead; timed entry is strict. The Palacios Nazaríes are the reason you flew to Spain. Mornings are the best light.
11:00
Generalife GardensThe sultan's summer gardens — terraced fountains, cypress-lined paths and rose pergolas above the city.
15:00
Albaicín neighbourhoodThe ancient Moorish quarter climbs the hillside opposite the Alhambra. The Mirador de San Nicolás offers the definitive view.
20:00
Cave flamenco in SacromonteFlamenco performed in the gypsy cave dwellings of Sacromonte — rawer and more intimate than any stage show.
22:30
Free tapas cultureIn Granada, every drink comes with a free tapa. Stroll Calle Navas and order cold beer — the city feeds you without asking.

Local tips

🍷 Lunch is the main meal in Spain — eaten between 2pm and 4pm. Menú del día (€12–15) is three courses with wine. It's the best value meal in Europe.
🎟 Book the Alhambra at least 3 weeks ahead. Tickets sell out completely. The Parador de Granada is the only hotel inside the complex — worth splurging.
🌙 Spaniards eat dinner at 10pm. Don't show up to a restaurant before 9pm if you want to blend in and get the best table.
🚆 Renfe's Tarjeta Dorada or advance fares can cut rail costs by 50%. Always book AVE tickets online — station prices are higher.
🕺 In Granada, every drink comes with a free tapa — no asking required. Order wine, not beer, for the better tapas.
