An Archipelago Like No Other

Venturing inland, one can explore local island communities, bustling fish markets, and observe local artisans, or partake in lively celebrations such as Eid or Maldivian Independence Day. The more adventurous can embark on a seaplane tour for a bird's eye view of the stunning atolls, or dive into the depths of the Indian Ocean to discover an underwater world teeming with life. The Maldives also boasts world-class resorts where one can unwind with luxurious spa treatments, indulge in gourmet cuisine, and experience the tranquility of overwater villas with panoramic ocean views. The Maldives, a captivating blend of luxury and culture, promises to be your unforgettable escape.

⊙ Top Things to See
Sunrise over the surf from a palm-draped coral-sand beach.
The mesmerising underwater world just meters from your beach towel.
The mosques and bustling fish market in Malé, the pocket-sized Maldivian capital.
Laid-back villages and the ruins of the British WWII air base on Gan Island.

Eat

  • garudia (smoked-fish soup)
  • hedika – delicious, spicy fish-based snacks

Drink

⊙ Top things to do
Dive or snorkel on spectacular thilas and giris (isolated reefs) and kandus (deepwater channels).
