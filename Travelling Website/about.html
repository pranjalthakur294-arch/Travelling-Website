Our Story

When a destination thrives, so does your experience there.

Lonely Planet Journeys was born from the idea that travellers and the destinations they visit deserve better. The travel industry is broken — most trips send money out of the places you explore, rather than into them.

We believe travel should be built on local insight, created and sold directly from the destination itself. That's why we connect you with local experts who live, breathe, and love their corner of the world.

Every trip planned through us keeps money in local communities, supports local guides, and creates experiences you simply can't find anywhere else.

Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about planning a trip with Lonely Planet Journeys.

Every trip is completely bespoke. Your local expert will design a full itinerary including accommodation recommendations, transport, activities, restaurant picks, and day-by-day guidance — all based on your personal preferences. They can also handle all the booking logistics if you'd like, or simply advise and let you book independently.
All of our local experts go through a thorough application and interview process. We check credentials, review past work, and require proof of local knowledge and experience. Many are professional guides, hospitality specialists, or long-term residents who are deeply embedded in their communities.
Absolutely. Your first discovery call with a local expert is completely free and comes with no obligation to book anything. It's a chance for you both to see if you're a good fit and to start discussing what your ideal trip might look like.
Planning fees vary by expert and destination. Most charge a modest trip planning fee (typically between $100–$350 USD), which covers the design of your complete bespoke itinerary. Actual travel costs — accommodation, transport, activities — are additional and booked separately. You'll always see clear pricing before committing.
Your expert will refine the itinerary through rounds of feedback until you're genuinely happy before any booking takes place. If you're unsatisfied with the planning service itself, our team will work with you to find a resolution — including a full refund of the planning fee if appropriate.

When you fill in your trip request, simply let us know who's coming and any special considerations (like travelling with young kids, elderly parents, or accessibility needs), and we'll match you with the right expert.
Yes. All bookings made through Lonely Planet Journeys are financially protected. We recommend travellers also take out comprehensive travel insurance, and your expert can advise on appropriate options for your destination.
